## 📌 Project Overview
This capstone project investigates **Shri Balaji Medical Store**, a B2C retail pharmacy located in rural Haryana, India[cite: 2, 3]. Operating primarily on manual records, the store faced critical operational hurdles including frequent stockouts of fast-moving generics (e.g., Paracetamol), overstocking of slow-moving items (e.g., Oxytetracycline), low sales visibility for high-margin products (e.g., Betadine), and risks associated with unmanaged medicine expiration dates[cite: 2, 3]. 

Using transaction-level data analysis through Microsoft Excel[cite: 2], this project provides data-driven recommendations to optimize inventory control, reduce expiry waste, and boost overall profitability[cite: 3].

## 📊 Key Findings & Statistics

* **Average Daily Revenue:** ₹1,033.30 (ranging from a minimum of ₹328 to a peak of ₹8,820)[cite: 1]
* **Top Revenue Generator:** Betadine 10g Ointment (₹111,840)[cite: 1]
* **Top Quantity Driver:** Crocin 500mg Tablet (1,022 units sold)[cite: 1]
* **ABC/Pareto Analysis:** 'A' category items account for a major 80% of total store revenue, indicating that a focused inventory approach is vital[cite: 1].
* **Expiry Risk:** Identified ₹53,061 in potential losses tied to unmanaged near-expiry or expired inventory, highlighting an urgent need for automated batch tracking[cite: 1].

## 🛠️ Tools & Methodology

* **Primary Tool:** Microsoft Excel (used for data cleaning, pivot tables, descriptive statistics, and visualization charts without requiring complex programming overhead)[cite: 2].
* **Analytical Techniques Applied:**
  * **Data Cleaning & Standardization:** Standardizing dates (`DD-MM-YYYY`), handling missing values, filtering duplicates, and aligning medicine naming conventions[cite: 1].
  * **Descriptive Statistics:** Calculating means, medians, modes, standard deviations, and variance for transaction quantities and amounts[cite: 2].
  * **ABC Analysis:** Segmenting inventory into revenue tiers to prioritize stocking levels[cite: 1].
  * **Expiry Loss Modeling:** Quantifying financial risk from products exceeding shelf life[cite: 1].

## 🚀 Strategic Recommendations

1. **Automated Inventory Thresholds:** Implement reorder points for top-selling medicines to eliminate stockouts by up to 90%[cite: 1].
2. **Demand Forecasting:** Utilize historical sales trends to predict seasonal surges (e.g., winter illnesses or local market events)[cite: 1].
3. **Targeted Promotions & Bundling:** Train staff to upsell high-margin items like Betadine and bundle slow-moving stock to improve cash flow[cite: 1].
4. **Expiry Management Protocol:** Institute quarterly shelf audits and markdown strategies for near-expiry products to drastically lower waste[cite: 1].
