## 📌 Project Overview
This capstone project investigates **Shri Balaji Medical Store**, a B2C retail pharmacy located in rural Haryana, India[cite: 2, 3]. Operating primarily on manual records, the store faced critical operational hurdles including frequent stockouts of fast-moving generics (e.g., Paracetamol), overstocking of slow-moving items (e.g., Oxytetracycline), low sales visibility for high-margin products (e.g., Betadine), and risks associated with unmanaged medicine expiration dates.

Using transaction-level data analysis through Microsoft Excel, this project provides data-driven recommendations to optimize inventory control, reduce expiry waste, and boost overall profitability.

## 📊 Key Findings & Statistics

* **Total Revenue (Jan–May 2025):** ₹292,759
* **Average Daily Revenue:** ₹1,033.30 (ranging from a minimum of ₹328 to a peak of ₹8,820)
* **Top Revenue Generator:** Betadine 10g Ointment (₹111,840)
* **Top Quantity Driver:** Crocin 500mg Tablet (1,022 units sold)
* **ABC/Pareto Analysis:** 'A' category items account for a major 80% of total store revenue, indicating that a focused inventory approach is vital.
* **Expiry Risk:** Identified ₹53,061 in potential losses tied to unmanaged near-expiry or expired inventory, highlighting an urgent need for automated batch tracking.

## 🛠️ Tools & Methodology

* **Primary Tool:** Microsoft Excel (used for data cleaning, pivot tables, descriptive statistics, and visualization charts without requiring complex programming overhead).
* **Analytical Techniques Applied:**
  * **Data Cleaning & Standardization:** Standardizing dates (`DD-MM-YYYY`), handling missing values, filtering duplicates, and aligning medicine naming conventions.
  * **Descriptive Statistics:** Calculating means, medians, modes, standard deviations, and variance for transaction quantities and amounts.
  * **ABC Analysis:** Segmenting inventory into revenue tiers to prioritize stocking levels.
  * **Expiry Loss Modeling:** Quantifying financial risk from products exceeding shelf life.

---

## 🚀 Strategic Recommendations

1. **Automated Inventory Thresholds:** Implement reorder points for top-selling medicines to eliminate stockouts by up to 90%.
2. **Demand Forecasting:** Utilize historical sales trends to predict seasonal surges (e.g., winter illnesses or local market events).
3. **Targeted Promotions & Bundling:** Train staff to upsell high-margin items like Betadine and bundle slow-moving stock to improve cash flow.
4. **Expiry Management Protocol:** Institute quarterly shelf audits and markdown strategies for near-expiry products to drastically lower waste.
